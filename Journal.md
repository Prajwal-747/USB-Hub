## May 4

Designing Schematics and Laying Foundation for PCB

Today I started with Designing the Schematic and laying the base shape of the PCB. I have chosen a layout of 5 ports USB Hub. I have taken 3 USB C Ports, one of them as an upstream USB Port and the other two as Downstream USB Ports, i have also taken 2 USB A Ports as Downstream Ports. These ports were selected as they were an equal balance and also suited my needs.
I had originally chosen 3 USB A ports, but i did not like that the symmetry might not be good while using, hence i switched one of those ports to a USB C port.
